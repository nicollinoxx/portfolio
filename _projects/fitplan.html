About the project

FitPlan is a modular web application for managing personalized workout and diet plans. Users can build custom workout routines and meal plans, calculate BMI (Body Mass Index), BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) and per-meal calories, track their progress, and share copies of their plans with other users.

Built with Ruby on Rails 8 and PostgreSQL, the app relies on background jobs for processing and caching to keep the experience fast, with a Hotwire-powered interface for smooth, reactive interactions and a responsive UI built with Bootstrap.
